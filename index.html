The program

What it is, what we fund, how it works, and whether it's you.

Click a tab, drag the lens, or use ← →

Small, short-term grants for people already solving neighbourhood problems, who just need a push across the finish line.

  • Solve it where you live

    The people who live with a problem know it best. We back fixes led by the neighbourhood itself.

  • Borrow, don't reinvent

    If a fix worked in one neighbourhood, adapt it for yours. Good ideas are meant to travel.

  • Pass it on

    Every grant ends with a story or playbook, so the next neighbourhood can start where you left off.

What we fund

Fixes already underway, led by the neighbourhood, that could work elsewhere too.

  • Public spaces

    Parks, footpaths, play areas, bus stops

    Reclaim a corner, fix a footpath, light up a dark stretch, or turn a dumping spot into a place people stop and sit.

  • Waste

    Segregation, composting, clean streets

    Ward-level composting, cutlery banks, segregation drives, or keeping a black spot clean for good.

  • Water

    Lakes, drains, rainwater, flooding

    Rain gardens, recharge wells, drain clean-ups, or a lake group that needs equipment to keep going.

  • Trees and greening

    Street trees, gardens, shade

    Planting and caring for street trees, shade for bus stops, or a community garden on an unused plot.

  • Walking and mobility

    Safe streets, crossings, cycling

    Safer school routes, crossings, cycle days, or signage that helps people walk their neighbourhood.

  • Something else?

    If it fixes a real neighbourhood problem and the idea could travel, tell us about it.

01What is the NoTF BLR Small Grants Program?

It awards small, short-term grants to individuals, collectives and organisations who are solving neighbourhood problems in Bengaluru.

02Who can apply?

Individuals, informal collectives (such as resident groups) and registered organisations working on a specific neighbourhood problem in Bengaluru.

03What will you fund?

Work that helps you finish a neighbourhood fix: materials, small equipment, local labour, events and outreach. We prefer ideas already underway, and ideas that could work in another neighbourhood too.

04What is the grant value and duration?

Between ₹50,000 and ₹2,00,000, for up to 6 months.

05How many grants are available?

About 10 in this round.

06What does the application process look like?

Apply online, have a short conversation with us, and hear our decision within about six weeks. Applications are reviewed as they come in.

07What are the reporting requirements?

No interim reports. At the end, a story or playbook of your fix and a fund utilisation certificate.
